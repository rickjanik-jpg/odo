Odo

Every work mile, logged automatically.

Odo notices when you drive and asks if it was for work. Your miles and tax deduction add up on their own. Free automatic tracking, no subscription, no ads.

  • Automatic. Drives are recorded without pressing anything.
  • One tap to label. “Was this drive for work?” Tap Work or Personal right on the notification.
  • Shifts. Start a shift and every drive counts as work. Works with Siri.
  • Expenses. Log parking, tolls and your phone bill, with receipt photos.
  • Private. Your trips stay on your phone. No account needed.

Built by a delivery driver who got tired of paying a subscription to track miles.
