Odo

Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Odo is a mileage tracker that keeps your data on your phone. We don’t have accounts or servers that store your information, and we never sell, share or look at your trips.

What Odo uses, and where it stays

  • Location. Odo uses your phone’s location to detect drives and measure miles, including when the app is closed if you allow “Always.” Routes are saved only on your phone.
  • Motion & Fitness. Odo uses your phone’s motion sensors to notice when you start and stop driving. This information is processed on your phone and isn’t saved or sent anywhere.
  • Trips, labels and settings. Dates, times, miles, routes, work or personal labels and your settings are stored only on your phone.
  • Expenses and receipt photos. Amounts, categories, notes and photos you add are stored only on your phone. Odo uses the camera or photo library only when you choose to add a receipt.

What we collect

Nothing. Odo has no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We (the developer) don’t receive your location, trips, expenses, photos or any other personal information.

Other services Odo relies on

  • Maps. To show a trip’s route on iPhone, Odo asks Apple Maps for a map picture of that area. Apple handles those requests under Apple’s privacy policy. The Android version may load map images from a third-party map provider in the same way.
  • Tips and purchases. Optional tips are processed by Apple (or Google on Android). We don’t receive your name, email or payment details, only a record that a purchase happened.
  • Backups you save. When you save a backup, you choose where it goes, such as Files, iCloud Drive or email. That copy is then handled by the service you picked.

Your choices

  • You can turn off location, motion or notification access at any time in your phone’s Settings.
  • You can edit or delete any trip or expense in the app.
  • Deleting Odo deletes all of its data from your phone. Save a backup first if you want to keep it.

Children

Odo is meant for adults who drive for work. It isn’t directed to children under 13, and it doesn’t knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the effective date above. If Odo ever starts sending data off your phone, for example an optional cloud backup, we’ll describe it here and ask before turning it on.
