Common questions

Odo didn’t record a drive

Open your phone’s Settings → Odo and check that Location is set to Always with Precise Location on, and that Motion & Fitness is allowed. Don’t swipe Odo away in the app switcher, because then iPhone may not wake it for your next drive. You can add a missed drive with + Add a trip by hand at the bottom of the Trips screen.

The miles look wrong

Tap the trip and fix the Miles field. GPS can drift in parking garages and dense downtowns. If it keeps happening, email me the date and rough route.

How do I log every drive as work during my shift?

Tap Start shift on the Trips screen, or say “Hey Siri, start shift in Odo.” Drives are logged as work until you end the shift or 12 hours pass. In Settings you’ll find steps to start a shift automatically when you open a work app like Dasher.

How do I bring in miles from before I used Odo?

Put them in a spreadsheet with a Date column and a Miles column (Purpose is optional), save it as CSV, and use Settings → Past trips → Import from a spreadsheet.

How do I keep my data safe or move to a new phone?

Settings → Backup → Save creates one file with all your trips, expenses and receipt photos. Keep it in Files or iCloud Drive. On the new phone, install Odo and use Restore from a backup.

What can I deduct?

Odo estimates your deduction with the IRS standard mileage rate (72.5¢ a mile for 2026). That rate already covers gas, oil, repairs, tires, insurance and car washes, so don’t add those as expenses too. Parking, tolls and the work share of your phone bill can be added separately. Odo is a record-keeping tool, not tax advice.

Does Odo cost anything?

Automatic tracking is free, with no ads. You can leave an optional tip in Settings → Support Odo.
